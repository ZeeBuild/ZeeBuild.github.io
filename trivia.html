How to play

Two minutes to learn.

  1. 1

    Draw a card

    One player flips a card and keeps the screen to themselves. They read the first two buzz-words out loud.

  2. 2

    Ask ten questions

    Everyone else asks yes-or-no questions. Tap a stop on the counter for each one. The card shows three clues and six facts if the table gets stuck.

  3. 3

    Try the bonus

    Name the answer to keep the card, then take the bonus question for a head start on the next one. First to seven cards wins.
